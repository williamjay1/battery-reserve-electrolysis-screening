# Battery reserve energy screening for electrolysis

Reproducibility materials for **Conservative distribution screening of battery reserve energy for electrolysis**, by **Junjie Zhang**, Shanghai International Studies University (ORCID 0009-0004-8821-4018).

This is an engineering comparison of energy allocation under frequency reserve activation. It distinguishes feasible schedules from optimistic energy bounds and certifies compression error. It does not establish hydrogen mass production, economic profitability or asset dispatch qualification.

## Contents

- `submission_revision_20260908/`: current optimization and compression methods, GB derived input, and frozen numerical results.
- `minute_revision_20260908/`: preceding source-processing methods, Belgian validated quarter input and historical comparison results. The current manuscript's main results are in the current folder above; preserve the sibling layout.
- `provenance/`: source-file checksums, German daily-cache checksums and quality audit. Checksums identify excluded German inputs; they are not the observations themselves.
- `DATA_SOURCES.md`: licenses, transformations and access limitations.
- `REPRODUCIBILITY.md`: offline checks and source-dependent full analysis.
- `MANIFEST.json`: release file sizes and SHA256 hashes.

**Public-data boundary:** this release includes Belgian and GB derived data. German native observations and lossless daily caches are not redistributed.
